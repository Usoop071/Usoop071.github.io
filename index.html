I am Utsab, a Class 11 student at NIST College, Kathmandu. I'm drawn to the layers beneath technology — how systems communicate, how security breaks (and holds), how a few lines of C can command a machine.

I explore Linux not just as a tool but as a philosophy of openness. Kali Linux is my playground for understanding how attackers think — because you can't defend what you don't understand.

Outside the terminal, you'll find me studying calculus, playing chess, or on the badminton court. My goal: become highly skilled in technology while never stopping to build and learn.
